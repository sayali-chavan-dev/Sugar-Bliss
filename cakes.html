- A cake is a sweet baked dessert usually made from flour, sugar, eggs, butter or oil, milk and baking ingredients. It is baked in an oven until soft, fluffy and delicious.

Cakes are commonly enjoyed during birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, festivals and other special celebrations.

Cakes can have different flavours, shapes, sizes, colours and decorations.


1. Fresh Cream Cakes

Freshly Made, Creamily Delicious, Perfectly Sweet! ❤️

Fresh Cream Cakes are soft, light and fluffy cakes decorated with freshly whipped cream instead of heavy buttercream or fondant.

They are usually made with soft sponge layers filled with whipped cream, fresh fruits, chocolate or flavoured fillings.

These cakes are known for their smooth creamy texture, light sweetness and refreshing taste.
They are perfect for birthdays, anniversaries, weddings and all kinds of celebration

2. Cheese Cakes

Rich, Creamy & Simply Irresistible! ❤️

A Cheesecake is a rich, creamy and delicious dessert made primarily with cream cheese, sugar and other ingredients. It usually has a buttery biscuit crust at the bottom and a smooth, creamy cheese filling on top.

Cheesecakes can be baked or prepared without baking. They are known for their velvety texture, slightly tangy flavour and perfectly balanced sweetness.

3. Bento Cakes

Small Cakes, Sweet Surprises, Endless Happiness! ❤️
Our Bento Cakes are little bundles of happiness made especially for your special moments. ❤️
Perfect for birthdays, anniversaries, surprises or simply satisfying your sweet cravings, these mini cakes are freshly baked and beautifully decorated with love.
Choose your favourite flavour, add a cute message and personalize your design. Small in size, big in taste and full of sweetness!
